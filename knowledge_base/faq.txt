CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

ACCOUNT
- Customers can create an account with a valid email address and password.
- If a customer forgets their password, they should use the Forgot Password link.
- Account email changes can be requested from account settings.
- Verification may be required after changing an email address.

ORDERS
- Customers can view current and previous orders from the Orders section.
- An order that has not shipped can normally be cancelled from its order details.
- Once an order has entered shipping, cancellation may no longer be possible.
- Customers should contact support for cases that require manual intervention.

SHIPPING
- Standard shipping normally takes 3-5 business days after dispatch.
- Express shipping normally takes 1-2 business days after dispatch.
- Tracking information becomes available after the carrier receives the package.
- Delivery dates may change because of carrier delays, weather, holidays,
  or an incorrect delivery address.

RETURNS AND REFUNDS
- Eligible products can normally be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Returned products should normally be unused and in their original packaging.
- Refunds are initiated after the returned product passes inspection.
- Bank or card processing time may add several business days after a refund
  is issued.
- Personalized and final-sale items may have different return rules.

PAYMENTS
- Available payment methods are displayed during checkout.
- For a failed payment, check card details, billing address, and available funds.
- Customers must never send a full card number, CVV, password, or OTP to support.

SECURITY
- Support agents never need a customer's password, full card number, CVV, or OTP.
- If an account may be compromised, the customer should change the password
  and contact support through the official support channel.

ESCALATION
- Escalate billing disputes, suspected fraud, account compromise,
  repeated delivery failures, and cases requiring private account records.
- The AI support agent must not invent policies.
- If the knowledge base does not answer a question, recommend human support.
